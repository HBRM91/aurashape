## Aurashape's ASO Advantage

1. **"Free" in title/search terms** — most competitors have paywalls
2. **"Privacy-first" angle** — unique in a market full of data sellers
3. **All-in-one** — food + fasting + workout + progress = higher retention
4. **Science-backed** — differentiates from bro-science apps
5. **EU-hosted** — GDPR advantage for European users
6. **No ads** — rare in free apps

## App Store Title Strategy

**Title**: Aurashape — Food Tracker, Fasting & Workout Log

**Subtitle**: Free calorie counter & health tips

This packs "food tracker", "fasting", "workout log", "free", "calorie counter", and "health" into the 30-char title + 30-char subtitle — the two highest-weighted ASO fields.
