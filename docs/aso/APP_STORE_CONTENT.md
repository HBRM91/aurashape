## Long Description — English (for both App Store and Play Store)

### Aurashape — Your Free Health Companion

Track your nutrition, master intermittent fasting, log workouts, and learn science-backed health tips — all in one beautifully simple app. 100% free. No ads. Your data stays yours.

**Why Aurashape?**

Most health apps lock features behind paywalls or sell your data. Aurashape doesn't. We're building a community of health-conscious people with a simple promise: free forever, privacy from day one.

**What you can do:**

**FOOD TRACKING**
- Log meals in seconds with barcode scanning or smart search
- Search 2 million+ foods via Open Food Facts
- See your macros at a glance with beautiful progress rings
- Create custom foods and save your favorites
- Hit your protein, carb, and fat targets daily

**INTERMITTENT FASTING**
- Choose from 7 fasting plans (14:10 to 20:4, 5:2, 6:1, Custom)
- Beautiful animated timer shows your progress
- Get reminders when your fast ends
- Track your fasting history with a monthly calendar

**WORKOUT TRACKING**
- Browse 100+ exercises across 5 categories
- Log sets, reps, and weight as you train
- Track your total volume and personal records
- See detailed workout summaries with duration and volume

**BODY & PROGRESS**
- Log your weight and see the trend over time
- Track 11 body measurements (neck to calves)
- Take progress photos (front, side, back)
- Everything stays private — encrypted and for your eyes only

**SCIENCE-BACKED TIPS**
- Daily health tips with real scientific citations
- Deep-dive articles on nutrition, fasting, and training
- No bro-science. Every tip cites a peer-reviewed study.

**COMMUNITY**
- Join weekly challenges with leaderboards
- Share your favorite recipes with the community
- Discuss fasting, nutrition, and workouts in the forum
- View member profiles and connect with like-minded people
- Like, comment, and engage with the community

**PRIVACY BY DESIGN**
- All data encrypted at rest and in transit
- Hosted exclusively in the EU (Frankfurt)
- We never sell your data. We never see your meals.
- No advertising SDKs. No third-party trackers (unless you opt in).
- Full GDPR compliance — export or delete your data anytime

**Free. Forever.**

No subscriptions. No premium tiers. No feature gates. We're building Aurashape as a community-first product, not a monetization machine.

Download Aurashape and start your health journey today.
