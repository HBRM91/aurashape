# Aurashape — 12-Month Roadmap

*Version 1.0 — July 2026 | 3-Person Team*

---

## Phase 0: Pre-Launch (Month 1 — August 2026)

**Goal:** Foundation. App runs, auth works, DB deployed.

| Week | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| W1-2 | Sprint 0: Expo scaffold, Supabase DB, auth, privacy consent, onboarding wizard |
| W3-4 | Sprint 1: Food diary core (search, barcode, custom foods, macro dashboard, habits) |

---

## Phase 1: MVP Build (Month 2-3 — Sept-Oct 2026)

**Goal:** All P0 features. Ready for beta.

| Week | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| W5 | Sprint 2: Intermittent fasting (timer, plans, notifications) |
| W6-7 | Sprint 3: Workout tracking (exercise library, logger, history, post-workout summary) |
| W8 | Sprint 4: Body & progress (weight, measurements, progress photos) |
| W9 | Sprint 5: Science content engine (daily tips, articles, "The Science" badges) |
| W10-11 | Sprint 6: Community features (profiles, challenges, recipe sharing, forum, comments) |
| W12 | Sprint 7: Platform polish (email capture, dark mode, push notifications, offline sync, analytics, GDPR compliance) |

---

## Phase 2: Beta Launch (Month 4 — November 2026)

**Goal:** Stable app. Real users. First community.

| Week | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| W13-14 | Internal beta (team + 10 friends). Bug fixes. Performance. |
| W15-16 | Closed beta (50-100 users). Feedback. Community seeding. |
| W17-18 | App Store + Google Play submission. ASO. Launch marketing. |

---

## Phase 3: Growth (Month 5-8 — Dec 2026-Mar 2027)

**Goal:** 1,000 → 2,500 users. Community self-sustaining.

- AI Food Photo Recognition (P1)
- Streaks & achievements (P1)
- Apple Health / Google Fit sync
- Custom workout routines
- Fasting history calendar
- Newsletter hits 500 subscribers
- Aurabiosens product teasers begin in newsletter

---

## Phase 4: Community Maturity (Month 9-12 — Apr-Jul 2027)

**Goal:** 5,000 → 10,000 users. Ready for product launch.

- German localization (i18n)
- Curated recipe library + meal plan generator
- Shopping list generator
- Advanced challenges (team-based)
- Referral engine
- Power user moderation program
- Aurabiosens launch announcement to email list

---

## Phase 5: Product Launch (Month 12+ — July 2027+)

- Aurabiosens product hits market
- Aurashape community = first customers
- App update: Aurabiosens product tracking + shop tab
- Community launch discount codes
- Wearable integration exploration

---

## Key Decisions

| Decision | Status |
|---|---|
| Community in MVP (not deferred) | ✅ P0 |
| Science content engine in MVP | ✅ P0 |
| AI features deferred to post-launch | ✅ P1 |
| No shop/funnel until Aurabiosens launches | ✅ Removed |
| Privacy-first from Day 1 | ✅ Competitive advantage |
| Email capture optional | ✅ GDPR + trust |
| Launch in English, localize later | ✅ Speed to market |
