Aurashape
Your free, privacy-first health companion

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who We Are

Aurashape is a free health companion app operated from the European Union. Our infrastructure is hosted exclusively on EU-based servers (Frankfurt, Germany) via Supabase.

Contact: privacy@aurashape.app

2. What Data We Collect

Account Data

Email address (for authentication), OAuth provider ID (Google/Apple sign-in), and display name.

Health Data (Optional, User-Provided)

Goal preferences, biological sex, date of birth, height, weight, activity level, dietary preference, fasting plan, food diary entries, workout logs, body measurements, and progress photos.

Usage Data (Optional, Opt-In)

Anonymous usage analytics via PostHog (EU-hosted). No personally identifiable information is included.

Device Data

Push notification token (if enabled) and crash reports via Sentry.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT track your location
  • We do NOT access your contacts, calendar, messages, or other apps
  • We do NOT sell your data to anyone. Ever
  • We do NOT use your data for advertising
  • We do NOT share your health data with third parties
  • We do NOT even see your meals — all food logging is processed on-device first

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Newsletter subscription, analytics opt-in, push notifications
  • Performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Providing the health tracking service you requested
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Crash reporting for app stability, basic account security

5. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) in the EU (eu-central-1, Frankfurt) and encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Progress photos are stored in private Supabase Storage buckets with Row-Level Security — only you can access your photos.

6. Data Retention

  • Active account: Data retained while account exists
  • Deleted account: All data permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Newsletter: Unsubscribed immediately on request
  • Crash reports: Retained for 90 days

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

  • Access: Export your data in JSON format via Settings → Export My Data
  • Rectification: Edit any profile field or re-run onboarding
  • Erasure: Delete your account — all data cascade-deleted within 30 days
  • Portability: JSON export includes all profile, diary, fasting, and workout data
  • Withdraw consent: Toggle off analytics, newsletter, or push notifications anytime
  • Object: Contact privacy@aurashape.app
